ROYAL SNACK HOUSE

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Royal Snack House may handle information submitted through its online store.

Information collected

When you create an account or place an order, the store may process your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order details, account preferences and support messages.

How information is used

  • To create and manage customer accounts.
  • To process orders, delivery and refunds.
  • To provide order updates and customer support.
  • To maintain store security, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations.
  • To send promotional messages only where you have opted in or otherwise permitted by law.

Service providers

Information may be processed by service providers supporting hosting, database, authentication, delivery and payment services, only as needed to operate the store.

Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect account and order information. Information is retained only as needed for business, legal and accounting purposes.

Your choices

You may update eligible account information through your customer account or contact the store through the support details published on the website. Do not submit payment card PINs, passwords or OTPs to customer support.
