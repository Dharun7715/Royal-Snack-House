Cancellation & Refund Policy

Order cancellation

Customers may request cancellation any time before the order is dispatched. Once dispatched, cancellation may not be possible.

How to request cancellation

Use the order support channel shown on the website when it becomes available and provide your order number and registered phone number.

Refunds

For eligible cancelled orders and approved refunds, the refund will be initiated within 2–3 working days. The time taken for the amount to appear in the original payment account may depend on the bank or payment provider.

Damaged, incorrect or missing items

Please report any damaged, incorrect or missing item promptly after delivery, with the order number and clear photographs where relevant. The team will review the issue and communicate the available resolution.

Non-refundable situations

Orders already dispatched cannot ordinarily be cancelled under the pre-dispatch cancellation rule. Any exception will be assessed case by case in accordance with applicable law.
