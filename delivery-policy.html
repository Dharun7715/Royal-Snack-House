ROYAL SNACK HOUSE

Delivery Policy

Royal Snack House currently plans to accept delivery orders for serviceable addresses across Tamil Nadu, India.

Delivery charges

  • ₹40 delivery charge for orders below ₹500.
  • Free delivery for orders of ₹500 or more.
  • No minimum order value.

Estimated delivery time

Estimated delivery is 2–3 days from order confirmation. Delivery can take longer for remote locations, high-demand periods, weather disruptions or courier delays. We will communicate material delays when contact details are available.

Serviceability

Delivery is subject to courier availability at the address entered during checkout. Please provide a complete address and reachable mobile number.

Order updates

Order confirmation and tracking/status updates are provided through the customer account and available order communication channels.
